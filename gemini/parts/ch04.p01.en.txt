The war on the prosperous peasant only intensified the

Exploitation, employs hired help or derives an income

The financial and manufacturing straits of the Soviet 
Government added to the agrarian crisis. Gold had ceased 
To circulate, and paper money did not tempt the peasant. 
He saw value chiefly in goods, and whenever he sold grain, 
A calf, a pig, he wanted to buy something. But the Soviets

Less of his surplus. He consumed more himself, and the 
Remainder he kept for future advantage.
