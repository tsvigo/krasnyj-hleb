The question of the division of income is still in a state 
Of experimentation. The Moscow kolhoztsentr (national 
Kolkhoz body) has worked out a general scheme for the year 
Of 1930. According to this scheme five percent of the 
Income from crops and stock, including dairy products, is 
To be set aside as dividends on the properties that the 
Individual members have turned in to the kolkhoz- Taxes, 
Insurance, reserves for possible mishaps, and for seed are 
Figured out on the basis of prevailing conditions and needs 
Of the kolkhoz, and provided for accordingly. These sums are 
Then deducted from the gross income and ten percent of 
The remainder is appropriated for the indivisible fund; five

The support of the old and the disabled. Special needs that 
May arise may be taken care of by special appropriations. 
The balance, after all above items, are covered, is distributed 
As wages and profits among the members.
